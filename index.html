Privacy Policy — JakinsCraft Apps

Effective: September 30, 2026 · Developer: JakinsCraft (Pappy Akindipe)

This policy applies to all mobile apps published by JakinsCraft, including Budget Buddy, Expense Tracker, E1 Print Pricing, Server Monitor, Hydro Smart Saver, Birthday Wishes, Send Money Home Compare, and Canadian Citizenship Test Practice.

Data we collect

None. Our apps do not create accounts, do not ask for your name, email, or any personal information, and do not send your data to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

Anything you enter into an app (budgets, expenses, contacts, settings, quiz statistics) is stored only on your own device, in the app's private local storage. It never leaves your phone unless you explicitly share or export it yourself (for example, using the share sheet or exporting a CSV). Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Network use

  • Send Money Home Compare fetches public mid-market exchange rates from a public rate API so it can convert currencies. No personal information is sent with these requests.
  • Server Monitor connects only to the server address you enter in the app's own settings (your own home server). We never see or receive this traffic.
  • All other apps work fully offline.

Analytics, ads, and trackers

Our apps contain no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Children

Our apps do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
